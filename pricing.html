CLARITY FIRST

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about plans, billing, implementation, and enterprise scalability.

Can I try the ERP before paying?

Yes, all plans include a fully functional 14-day free trial pre-loaded with industry sample data. You can invite your team members, test core modules, and configure custom workflows with zero credit card required.

Can I change my plan or add users later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade your tier or add modular user packs at any time directly from your admin billing portal. Prorated charges will apply automatically for the remainder of your billing cycle.

Do you offer discounts for annual billing?

Yes, we offer a flat 20% discount on all our pricing tiers when you choose the annual billing cycle instead of monthly. This helps enterprises lock in better rates for long-term scalability.

Is implementation and onboarding included?

Our Enterprise plan includes a dedicated onboarding specialist and white-glove setup. Starter, Business, and Professional plans come with comprehensive documentation, live-chat support, and self-paced setup wizards.

How is our enterprise data secured?

We employ military-grade AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, and maintain strict SOC 2 Type II compliance. We also conduct regular third-party penetration testing to ensure zero-trust perimeter governance.

Do you provide custom ERP solutions & custom APIs?

Yes. Our Enterprise tier offers bespoke scoping, allowing our engineering team to build custom schemas, specialized integrations, and dedicated API endpoints tailored perfectly to your exact business logic.
