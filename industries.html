EXPERTISE & ADVISORY

Frequently Asked Questions by Industry Leaders

Clear answers on migration paths, security isolation, customization, and enterprise deployment.

How long does an enterprise migration from SAP, Oracle, or NetSuite typically take?

While traditional generic ERP replacements demand 18 to 36 months, AuraERP delivers phased deployments in as little as 14 to 45 days. Our automated data migration pipelines extract legacy schemas, reconcile historic general ledgers, and map transactional histories directly into our unified Data Fabric without downtime.

Can we customize schemas and business logic without breaking future platform updates?

Yes. AuraERP features a metadata-driven extensible engine. Domain-specific custom fields, validation triggers, and event brokers operate in isolated user spaces. When core kernel upgrades roll out, your bespoke workflows, reports, and API integrations remain 100% intact.

How does AuraERP handle sovereign data residency and multi-region mandates?

AuraERP supports localized multi-region database sharding. You can lock patient records to EU data centers (GDPR), financial ledgers to US-East (SOX), and defense projects to isolated FedRAMP High enclaves, all while viewing aggregated telemetry via a consolidated global executive dashboard.

What deployment models are supported: Public Cloud, Private VPC, or On-Premise?

We offer multi-tenant cloud with strict logical isolation, dedicated single-tenant VPC deployments on AWS/Azure/GCP, and hybrid-edge clusters for air-gapped manufacturing facilities requiring on-premise execution with asynchronous cloud synchronization.

What enterprise SLA support and advisory services are included?

Every enterprise client receives a 99.99% uptime SLA, 15-minute response times for critical telemetry disruptions, a dedicated Technical Account Manager, and ongoing architectural reviews with our senior solutions engineers.
