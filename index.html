FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know About ERPFlow

Straight answers to enterprise procurement, compliance, data migration, and deployment timelines.

How does ERPFlow handle data migration from legacy ERP systems like SAP or Oracle?

ERPFlow provides automated ETL connectors and bi-directional staging adapters specifically tuned for legacy SAP, Oracle NetSuite, and Microsoft Dynamics environments. A dedicated solutions engineering team supervises dry-run shadow ledgers to ensure zero system downtime and complete 1:1 financial data integrity during cutover.

Is ERPFlow compliant with international security and data privacy standards?

Yes. ERPFlow maintains continuous compliance with SOC-2 Type II, ISO 27001, GDPR, and HIPAA certifications. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest using AES-256 multi-region keys, and role-based permissions allow granular access enforcement at the individual ledger or warehouse level.

What is the typical enterprise deployment timeline?

Standard enterprise deployments complete within 6 to 12 weeks. Thanks to ERPFlow's modular architecture, organizations can activate individual pillars (such as Global Treasury or Warehouse Management) in parallel phased waves to realize immediate ROI without waiting for total stack migration.

Can ERPFlow support custom business workflows and regulatory jurisdictions?

Absolutely. ERPFlow features a visual low-code workflow engine, custom schema extensions, multi-currency ledger support, and 120+ localized tax compliance packs (including VAT, GST, and US Sales Tax automatic nexus computation).
