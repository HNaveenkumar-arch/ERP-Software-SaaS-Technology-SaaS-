ANSWERS & CLARITY

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about initiating a partnership with AuraERP.

Can I request a free ERP demo?

Yes, you can request a customized, live demonstration of our ERP platform. Our solutions architect will tailor the demo specifically to your industry workflows, showcasing features like automated reconciliation, inventory tracking, and executive dashboards.

Can the ERP be customized for my business?

Absolutely. AuraERP is built on a highly modular and extensible architecture. We offer bespoke scoping for custom integrations, specialized schemas, and automated rule engines that perfectly align with your unique organizational hierarchy.

Do you provide technical support?

All Enterprise clients receive a dedicated Technical Account Manager and 24/7 priority support with a 15-minute response SLA. Other tiers also include robust live-chat, comprehensive API documentation, and expert email support.

Can I integrate the ERP with other software?

Yes. AuraERP comes with over 200+ pre-built enterprise connectors (Salesforce, Shopify, Snowflake, etc.). We also provide native REST and GraphQL APIs for seamless bi-directional syncing with your legacy systems or proprietary tools.
